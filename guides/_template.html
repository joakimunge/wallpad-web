A Home Assistant dashboard for iPad

Updated 29 October 2026 · For iPadOS 16 or later

Hearth is an iPad app that turns your Home Assistant into a finished dashboard. Install it, sign in to your Home Assistant, tap once, and each of your areas gets its own page.

It runs on any iPad with iPadOS 16 or later, so a spare older iPad on the wall works too.

What you need

  • An iPad with iPadOS 16 or later.
  • Home Assistant running at home, on the same network as the iPad, or reachable at your own remote address.
  • A Home Assistant user to sign in with. It doesn’t have to be an admin. You can also use an address and a long-lived access token.
  • Areas in Home Assistant. Hearth makes one page per area, so the better your areas, the better your pages.

Set it up

  1. Install Hearth from the App Store and open it.
  2. Hearth looks for Home Assistant on your Wi-Fi. Pick yours, or enter its address.
  3. Sign in with your Home Assistant account, or use an access token instead.
  4. Tap Create my dashboard. If you’d rather choose, answer two short questions first: what you reach for most, and what you want to see at a glance.
  5. That’s it. To change a page later, long-press any card.

What you get

Home is the page you’ll see most: doors and windows left open, the next departures, a tile for each room, your scenes, the automations you run by hand, and what’s playing. Each room has its own page with every device you can control, the ones you use most first.

A light that’s on glows like a lamp, so there’s no On or Off to read. Climate, blinds, locks, cameras and speakers each get a card made for them.

On the wall

Put a spare iPad where everyone passes and it becomes the household screen.

  • When nobody’s using it, the dashboard fades to a clock, the weather and anything that needs attention. Those notices are read-only, so someone walking past can’t unlock a door.
  • The screen dims after a delay you choose and stays low at night, during hours you set.
  • A touch wakes it, or the front camera can when someone walks up. That runs on the iPad, and nothing is saved or sent.
  • You can make a separate dashboard for the wall, say one for the hallway and one for the kitchen.
  • To keep the iPad in Hearth, use iPadOS’s own Guided Access (Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access).

Limits

  • Hearth is a dashboard, not a replacement for Home Assistant. Devices, integrations and automations stay in Home Assistant.
  • iPadOS pauses apps in the background. Hearth is up to date while it’s open, and it isn’t an alarm or monitoring app.
  • Camera cards show snapshots that refresh, not live video.
  • Hearth decides how things look. You choose what’s on each page and how big each card is, not its colours or fonts.

Questions

Does Hearth work on an older iPad?

Yes, if it runs iPadOS 16 or later.

Does the wall iPad need an admin account?

No. Any Home Assistant user works. Things only an admin can do, like moving a device to another room, are only offered to admins.

Can two iPads show different dashboards?

Yes. You can keep more than one dashboard for the same home and pick one on each iPad.

Try it with your Home Assistant

Sign in, tap once, and your rooms are there.
