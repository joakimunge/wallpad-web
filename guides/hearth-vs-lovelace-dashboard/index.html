Hearth or a dashboard you build yourself

Updated 29 October 2026 · Compared with Home Assistant’s own dashboards, often called Lovelace

Build your own Home Assistant dashboard if you want to shape every card and show anything in your system, in any browser. Choose Hearth if you want a calm, finished dashboard on iPad and iPhone without designing or maintaining one.

They don’t exclude each other: Hearth doesn’t change your Home Assistant dashboards.

Side by side

Your own Home Assistant dashboardHearth
Getting started Home Assistant can generate a dashboard for you. Making it your own means adding views and cards, in the visual editor or in YAML. Sign in and tap once. Each area becomes a page, with what you use most up front.
How it looks Whatever you build: themes, layouts and community cards. One look, Paper, in light or dark. Lights that are on glow like a lamp.
What it can show Anything in Home Assistant, including history graphs, maps and settings. The everyday things: lights, climate, blinds, locks, alarm, media, cameras as snapshots, sensors, departures, appliances, scenes and automations. A custom card covers the rest.
Upkeep New devices go where you put them. Pages follow your areas. Set up again rebuilds them when your home changes.
Where it runs Any browser, and Home Assistant’s companion apps. A native app for iPad and iPhone, iOS 16 or later.
On a wall iPad Possible, though a kiosk setup usually needs extra tools, such as a kiosk browser app. Built in: standby with clock, weather and read-only notices, dimming and night hours.
Changing automations and settings Yes, it’s Home Assistant. No. That stays in Home Assistant.

When to build your own

  • You enjoy designing dashboards and want every card exactly so.
  • You need things Hearth doesn’t show, like history graphs, maps or Home Assistant settings.
  • The screen isn’t an iPad or iPhone.

When Hearth fits

  • You never got round to a dashboard you’re happy with, or yours needs fixing every time you add a device.
  • Other people in the house use it and need it to be obvious.
  • You want a wall iPad that looks finished without a kiosk setup.

Using both

Hearth reads Home Assistant and leaves your dashboards alone, so many setups use both: Hearth on the wall and on everyone’s phones, and a hand-built dashboard for the person who runs Home Assistant. You can also start Hearth from one of your existing dashboards instead of the automatic setup.

Questions

Is Hearth a Lovelace theme or custom card?

No. Hearth is a separate iPad and iPhone app. It talks to Home Assistant directly and draws its own dashboard; nothing is installed in Home Assistant.

Will Hearth change my existing dashboards?

No. Apart from controlling your devices, the only change Hearth makes in Home Assistant is giving a room to a device that has none, and only when an admin chooses it.

Can I make Hearth look like my own dashboard?

No. You choose what’s on each page and how big each card is; Hearth decides how it looks.
