A Home Assistant dashboard without YAML

Updated 29 October 2026 · iPad and iPhone

You don’t need YAML, or to place a single card. Hearth reads your Home Assistant’s areas, devices and how you use them, and builds the dashboard itself on iPad and iPhone.

You can still rename, resize or remove anything afterwards.

How Hearth decides what goes where

  1. Areas become pages. Each area in Home Assistant gets its own page, with every device in it that you can control.
  2. Devices get the card made for them. Hearth uses Home Assistant’s standard device types, so a light gets a lamp card, a thermostat a climate card and a speaker a music card, whatever brand they are.
  3. What you use goes first. Only what people switch by hand counts, so motion rules, schedules and lights switched as a group don’t skew it.
  4. Noise stays out. Diagnostic sensors, duplicates and technical names are left off the pages.
  5. Home gathers the rest. Doors left open, the next departures, a tile for each room, your scenes, the automations you run by hand, and what’s playing.

Getting a better result

Hearth relies on what Home Assistant already knows, so a little tidying there pays off everywhere.

  • Put devices in areas. In Home Assistant, open Settings, then Areas, labels & zones. A device without an area has no page to go on.
  • Give devices friendly names. Hearth shows the names Home Assistant has. You can also rename a card in Hearth without touching Home Assistant.
  • Place leftovers from Hearth. If you’re an admin, Hearth shows devices without a room and lets you put them in one. Apart from controlling your devices, that is the only change Hearth makes in Home Assistant.

What you can still change

Long-press a card to rename, resize or remove it, or let Magic layout tidy the page for you. If something has no card, make one with a name, an icon, a line of text and what a tap should do. It will look like every other card.

What Hearth won’t do

  • It won’t let you style cards. Hearth decides colours, fonts and spacing, so there’s nothing to design.
  • It won’t edit automations, add devices or change integrations. That stays in Home Assistant.
  • It won’t change your Home Assistant dashboards. You can keep using them alongside Hearth.

Questions

Do I need to know YAML at all?

Not for Hearth. You sign in and tap once. Anything you set up in Home Assistant itself is up to you.

What if my areas are a mess?

Hearth still makes a dashboard, but pages follow your areas. Tidy them in Home Assistant, then use Set up again in Hearth to rebuild the dashboards. Your sign-in stays.

Can I start from a dashboard I already have?

Yes. Instead of the automatic setup, you can start from one of your Home Assistant dashboards.
