A Home Assistant dashboard you don’t have to build

Hearth is an iPad and iPhone app for Home Assistant. Sign in, tap once, and each of your areas gets its own page, with the things you use most up front.

How does setup work?

Sign in to your Home Assistant and tap once. Hearth builds the dashboard from your areas, so there’s no YAML and no layout to plan.

Hearth looks for Home Assistant on your Wi-Fi, and you sign in with your Home Assistant account, or with any address and an access token.

Then tap once. Each of your areas becomes a page, with the things you reach for most up front. Only what people switch by hand counts, so motion rules, schedules and lights switched as a group don’t skew it.

If you’d rather choose, answer two short questions first: what you reach for most, and what you want to see at a glance.

  1. 1

    Hearth finds your Home Assistant. Sign in.

  2. 2

    Tap Create my dashboard. Hearth sorts everything into rooms.

  3. 3

    Every area has its page. That’s it.

What’s on the Home page?

Home is the page you’ll see most. Cards of the same kind sit together, and some only appear when there’s something to show.

  • Doors, windows and gates left open, and for how long.
  • The next bus, tram or train, from any integration that reports departures.
  • A tile for each room. Tap it to switch the lights, or open it for everything else.
  • Every whole-home scene, together on one card.
  • The automations and scripts you run by hand, one tap each.
  • What’s playing, and where.

How do lights look?

A light that’s on glows like a lamp, so there’s no On or Off to read.

Climate, blinds, locks, cameras and speakers each get a card made for them.

Cards press in under your finger, with light haptics you can turn off. Controls stay in the same place, so your hand learns where they are.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. The same home fits your phone, with Home, Rooms and Music along the bottom and a search for any room, device or scene.

Open any speaker for big artwork and simple controls, and move what’s playing to another speaker, using what your speakers already support.

When you leave the player, a small one stays with you around the dashboard.

Can it go on a wall?

Yes. Put a spare iPad up where everyone passes and it becomes the household screen.

When nobody’s using it, the dashboard fades to a clock, the weather and anything that needs attention. Those notices are read-only, so someone walking past can’t unlock a door.

The screen dims after a delay you choose and stays low at night, during hours you set. A touch wakes it, or the front camera can when someone walks up. That runs on the iPad, and nothing is saved or sent.

What can I change?

Hearth decides how things look, so there’s nothing to design. You decide what’s on each page.

Long-press a card to rename, resize or remove it, or let Magic layout tidy the page for you. If something has no card, make one with a name, an icon, a line of text and what a tap should do. It will look like every other card.

The Paper look follows your device’s light or dark mode, or you can keep it on one. And you can have more than one dashboard for the same home, say one for the hallway and one for the kitchen.

Where does my data go?

Hearth talks straight to your Home Assistant, at home or at your own remote address. There are no Hearth servers in between and no account to sign up for.

Your sign-in stays in the iOS Keychain on your device. Wake on motion is optional and runs on the device. No images are saved or sent. There are no analytics, ads or trackers, in the app or on this site.

More questions

Does Hearth replace Home Assistant?

No. Hearth is a dashboard for the Home Assistant you already run. Devices, integrations and automations stay in Home Assistant; Hearth shows them and lets you control them.

Do I need a Hearth account?

No. You sign in to your own Home Assistant, with your Home Assistant account or with an address and a long-lived access token. There is no Hearth account and no Hearth server.

Which devices does Hearth run on?

iPad and iPhone with iPadOS or iOS 16 or later, including older iPads that run iPadOS 16.

Does it work with my devices and integrations?

Hearth reads Home Assistant’s own areas, devices and standard entity types, so it isn’t tied to one brand or integration. If something has no card, you can make a custom card for it.

Does it work away from home?

Yes, when your Home Assistant is reachable from outside your home, at your own remote address. Hearth connects to the address you give it.

Can everyone in the household use it?

Yes. Each person can sign in with their own Home Assistant user, and it doesn’t have to be an admin. Things only an admin can do, like moving a device to another room, are only offered to admins.

What happens to my existing Home Assistant dashboards?

Nothing. Hearth doesn’t change your Home Assistant dashboards. You can also start from one of them instead of the automatic setup.

Does Hearth show live camera video?

No. Camera cards show snapshots that refresh, not live video.

Can Hearth alert me when something happens?

Hearth is not an alarm or monitoring app. It shows what needs attention while it’s open, but iOS pauses apps in the background, so use Home Assistant’s own notifications for alerts.
