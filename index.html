A Home Assistant dashboard you don’t have to build

Hearth is an iPad and iPhone app for Home Assistant. Sign in, tap once, and each of your areas gets its own page, with the things you use most up front.

Download on theApp Store Watch the tour

Needs iOS 16 or later and your own Home Assistant.

Setup takes about a minute

There’s no YAML and no layout to plan. Hearth looks for Home Assistant on your Wi-Fi, and you sign in with your Home Assistant account, or with any address and an access token.

Then tap once. Each of your areas becomes a page, with the things you reach for most up front. Only what people switch by hand counts, so motion rules, schedules and lights switched as a group don’t skew it.

If you’d rather choose, answer two short questions first: what you reach for most, and what you want to see at a glance.

One page for the whole house

Home is the page you’ll see most. Cards of the same kind sit together, and some only appear when there’s something to show.

  • Doors, windows and gates left open, and for how long.
  • The next bus, tram or train, from any integration that reports departures.
  • A tile for each room. Tap it to switch the lights, or open it for everything else.
  • Every whole-home scene, together on one card.
  • The automations and scripts you run by hand, one tap each.
  • What’s playing, and where.

A page for each room

A light that’s on glows like a lamp, so there’s no On or Off to read. Climate, blinds, locks, cameras and speakers each get a card made for them.

Cards press in under your finger, with light haptics you can turn off. Controls stay in the same place, so your hand learns where they are.

Full-screen music

Open any speaker for big artwork and simple controls. The Play on button moves what’s playing to another speaker, using what your speakers already support.

When you leave the player, a small one stays with you around the dashboard.

For a spare iPad on the wall

Put one up where everyone passes and it becomes the household screen.

When nobody’s using it, the dashboard fades to a clock, the weather and anything that needs attention. Those notices are read-only, so someone walking past can’t unlock a door.

The screen dims after a delay you choose and stays low at night, during hours you set. A touch wakes it, or the front camera can when someone walks up. That runs on the iPad, and nothing is saved or sent.

What you can change

Hearth decides how things look, so there’s nothing to design. You decide what’s on each page.

Long-press a card to rename, resize or remove it, or let Magic layout tidy the page for you. If something has no card, make one with a name, an icon, a line of text and what a tap should do. It will look like every other card.

The Paper look follows your device’s light or dark mode, or you can keep it on one. And you can have more than one dashboard for the same home, say one for the hallway and one for the kitchen.

Privacy

Hearth talks straight to your Home Assistant, at home or at your own remote address. There are no Hearth servers in between and no account to sign up for. Your sign-in stays in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Wake on motion is optional and runs on the device. No images are saved or sent. There are no analytics, ads or trackers.
