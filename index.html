A short tour

Calm by default.

No grid to draw and no cards to design. Just your home, in the order you live in it.

Setup

Ready in a minute.

No YAML, and no layout to plan.

  1. Find your home

    Hearth looks for Home Assistant on your Wi-Fi. Sign in with your Home Assistant account, or use any address with an access token.

  2. Tap once

    You get a page for each of your Home Assistant areas, with what you reach for most up front. Motion rules, schedules and lights switched as a group don't count as use, so it follows people, not automations.

  3. Or customize first

    Two short questions: what you reach for most, and what you want at a glance.

Home

One quiet page for the whole house.

Cards of the same kind sit together in calm blocks. Things show up when they matter and step aside when they don't.

Open now

Doors, windows and gates left open, and for how long. Only there while something is.

Departures

The next bus, tram or train from any integration that reports one.

Rooms

One tile per room. Tap to switch its lights; open it for everything else.

Scenes

Every whole-home scene, together on one card.

Buttons

The automations and scripts you run by hand, one tap each.

Music

What's playing, and where.

Rooms

Every room gets its own page.

Lights glow like the lamps they are. Climate, blinds, locks, cameras and speakers each get a card made for them.

  • Tactile. Cards press in under your finger, with gentle haptics you can turn off.
  • Quiet. A card that's on looks on. No labels to read.
  • Steady. Controls stay where your hand expects them.
Music

Music, full screen.

Open any speaker for big artwork and simple controls.

  • Play on. Move what's playing to another speaker, using what your speakers already support.
  • Always close. A small player follows you around the dashboard.
On the wall

Made to live on the wall.

Mount a spare iPad and Hearth becomes the household screen: useful up close, restful from across the room.

Ambient standby

When nobody's using it, the dashboard fades to a clock, the weather and anything that needs you. Notices are read-only, so a passer-by can't unlock a door.

Dims when it should

The screen dims after a delay you choose, and night dimming keeps it low during the hours you set.

Wakes when you're near

A touch wakes it. Or let the front camera notice you walking up; that happens on the iPad, and nothing is stored or sent.

Yours

Opinionated, and still yours.

Hearth takes care of the look, so there's nothing to design. Change what matters to you and skip the rest.

Edit any card

Long-press to rename, resize or remove it, or let Magic layout tidy a page for you.

Custom cards

For anything without a card: a name, an icon, one line of text and what a tap does. It looks like every other card.

Paper, light or dark

Warm paper and ink that follows your device, or stays light or dark.

More than one dashboard

Keep a few for the same home, like one for the hallway and one for the kitchen.

Privacy

Between you and your Home Assistant.

Straight to your home

Hearth talks directly to your Home Assistant, at home or at your own remote address. No servers in between.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Your sign-in stays in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Camera stays put

Wake on motion is optional and runs on the device. No images are saved or sent.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no trackers.
