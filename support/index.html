Questions

Do I need Home Assistant?

Yes. Hearth is a dashboard for a Home Assistant server you run yourself, at home or at your own remote address. Hearth doesn’t include one and has no cloud service. If your accessories are in Apple Home, you can connect that instead.

What do I need to run it?

An iPad or iPhone with iOS or iPadOS 16 or later, and a current version of Home Assistant. If something seems to be missing, update Home Assistant first.

How do I sign in?

Tap Find my Home Assistant. Hearth looks for it on your Wi-Fi, so allow local network access when iOS asks. Pick your home or type its address, then sign in with your Home Assistant account. You can use a long-lived access token instead: in Home Assistant, open your profile, then Security, then Long-lived access tokens.

Can I try it without Home Assistant?

Yes. Tap Try the demo on the first screen to look around a sample home. Nothing is connected, and you can leave the demo from Settings, under Connection.

A device is in the wrong room

Hearth makes a page for each area in Home Assistant. Change the device’s area in Home Assistant, then set the dashboard up again (see below) to rebuild its pages.

How do I start a dashboard over?

Open Settings, choose Connection and tap Disconnect. On the list of dashboards, tap the ⋯ button next to the dashboard and choose Set up again. Its pages and cards start fresh, and you stay signed in. Choose Remove instead to delete the dashboard; removing the last one for a home also signs you out.

Where is my sign-in stored?

In the iOS Keychain on your device. It is sent only to your Home Assistant; Hearth has no account or server of its own. You can also revoke it in Home Assistant, under your profile, Security, Refresh tokens.

Can it stay on the wall?

Yes. Turn on Ambient standby in Settings and the iPad fades to a clock when nobody is using it, and can stay dim at night. A touch wakes it, or the front camera can when someone walks up; that runs on the iPad, and no images are saved or sent. To keep Hearth on screen, use the iPad’s Guided Access.
