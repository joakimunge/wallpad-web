Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Hearth is a dashboard for your own Home Assistant. It has no user accounts, shows no ads and does not track you. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information about you and your home.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store or receive your personal data. We have no servers that your data passes through.
  • Hearth talks directly to the Home Assistant you connect it to, and nowhere else with your data.
  • There is no account to create and no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Camera-based wake on motion is optional, runs entirely on your device, and never stores or sends images.

Your Home Assistant connection

To find your home, Hearth can search your local network for Home Assistant. You can also enter an address yourself, local or remote. When you sign in, your Home Assistant issues the app a sign-in token, which is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. If you use a long-lived access token instead, it is stored the same way.

Hearth reads your home's areas, devices, states and recent history from your Home Assistant, and sends it the commands you give, such as turning on a light. This happens directly between your device and your Home Assistant.

Signing in uses a small page on this website (wallpad.app/auth/) only to tell Home Assistant where to return you afterwards. Your credentials and tokens never pass through this website.

What stays on your device

Your dashboards, card layouts, local names and settings are saved on your device. To put what you use most up front, Hearth keeps a small on-device tally of how often each device is switched by hand, per day, for up to 60 days. It records counts only, never who switched something. Removing a dashboard deletes its pages and cards from your device.

Apple Home

If you connect Apple Home instead of Home Assistant, Hearth uses Apple's HomeKit framework on your device to show and control your accessories. That information is used only for your dashboard and is not sent anywhere by Hearth.

Camera and wake on motion

If you turn on wake on motion for standby, Hearth uses the front camera to notice movement in the room. Each frame is reduced to a coarse grid of brightness values, compared with the previous one and discarded. Hearth does not record, save, upload or share any images or video. The camera runs only while that setting is on; otherwise it is not used at all.

Cameras in your home

Camera cards show images and video from your Home Assistant, loaded directly from it. Hearth does not keep recordings.

The demo

The demo home uses made-up sample data and is not connected to anything. Its sample camera pictures may be loaded from a public photo service; no information about you or your home is sent.

Data we collect

None. Hearth includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we operate no servers that receive your data.

Children's privacy

Hearth does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
