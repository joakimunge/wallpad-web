Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Hearth is an iPad and iPhone app that shows and controls your own Home Assistant, or your Apple Home. It has no user accounts, shows no ads and does not track you. This page explains what the app does with information about you and your home.

The short version

  • We don’t collect your data. Nothing you do in Hearth is sent to us, and we run no servers that your data passes through.
  • Hearth talks directly to the Home Assistant you connect it to.
  • There is no account to create, and no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Wake on motion is optional, runs on the device, and never saves or sends images.

Your Home Assistant connection

Hearth can look for Home Assistant on your local network, which is why iOS asks for local network access. You can also type an address yourself, at home or remote.

When you sign in with your Home Assistant account, your Home Assistant gives the app a sign-in token. If you use a long-lived access token instead, you paste it into the app. Either way, the token is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and is sent only to the Home Assistant it belongs to.

Hearth reads your areas, devices, their states and recent history from Home Assistant, and sends it the commands you give, such as turning on a light. This goes directly between your device and your Home Assistant.

Camera snapshots and album art are loaded from your Home Assistant. Some integrations point to pictures hosted elsewhere, such as album art from a music service; Hearth then loads the picture from that address, as a web browser would.

The sign-in page on this website

Before Home Assistant lets an app sign in, it checks where it may send you back afterwards. For Hearth, your Home Assistant reads that from a small public page on this website, wallpad.app/auth/. The page is static and hosted on GitHub Pages.

Your password, the sign-in code and your tokens never reach this website. They pass only between the app and your Home Assistant. GitHub, which hosts the site, may log the IP address that requests come from, as web servers do. We run no analytics on this website.

What stays on your device

Your dashboards, card layouts, the names you give things in the app, and your settings are saved on your device.

To put what you use most up front, Hearth keeps a small tally on the device of how often each device in your home is switched, and at what times of day, for up to 60 days. It records counts, never who switched something.

Removing a dashboard deletes its pages and cards from your device. Removing the last dashboard for a Home Assistant also deletes its sign-in token and that tally.

Apple Home

If you connect Apple Home, Hearth uses Apple’s HomeKit framework on your device to show and control your accessories. Hearth uses that information only for your dashboard and does not send it anywhere.

Camera and wake on motion

If you set standby to wake on motion, Hearth uses the front camera to notice movement in the room. Each frame is reduced to a coarse grid of brightness values, compared with the previous one, and discarded. Hearth does not record, save, upload or share any images or video. The camera runs only while standby is set to wake on motion; otherwise Hearth does not use it.

The demo

The demo home uses made-up data and is not connected to any home. Its sample camera pictures are fetched from Unsplash, a free photo service. That request contains a search phrase and the app’s own Unsplash key, nothing about you or your home; Unsplash sees your IP address, as any website would. Outside the demo, Hearth does not contact Unsplash.

Purchases

Any purchase in Hearth is handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details.

Data we collect

None. Hearth contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we run no servers that receive your data. There is nothing for us to sell or share.

Children’s privacy

Hearth does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
